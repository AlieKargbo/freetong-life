# Income Features

Track the first player income systems for Freetong Life. Jobs are the reliable starting income; gigs add flexible one-off work; businesses give players a longer-term investment goal.

## Status key

- `[ ]` Planned
- `[/]` In progress or needs validation
- `[x]` Implemented and verified

## Jobs and careers

- [/] Starter Ice Cream Cafe job: accept the job, start a shift at the cafe, complete three tasks, and receive SLE 25,000. Existing players keep their cafe job and shift progress when their old save is restored. Verify in game.
- [/] Add more workplaces and career paths with different tasks and pay: Fish Grill Server (SLE 30,000 base) and Surf Shop Assistant (SLE 22,000 base) are implemented alongside the cafe role. Verify each venue flow in game.
- [/] Add career progression, promotions, and clear requirements: rank advances after 3, 8, and 15 shifts, with 5% additional pay per promotion. Hustle level 2 adds 10%. Verify promotion and payout values in game.
- [/] Show shift progress, pay, and career status in the Jobs app and HUD. Verify on narrow mobile and desktop layouts.
- [/] Save accepted jobs, active career, in-progress shift, task index, shifts, and career earnings in the existing game save. Verify logout and cross-device restore with a signed-in account.

## Gigs

- [ ] Add a Gig Board with short, optional jobs players can accept and complete.
- [ ] Start with a small set of gigs such as deliveries, errands, and event help.
- [ ] Give each gig clear steps, a time or effort estimate, and a payout shown before acceptance.
- [ ] Add cooldowns or rotating availability to keep payouts balanced.
- [ ] Save active and completed gigs so progress is consistent across sessions.

## Business ownership

- [ ] Let players view businesses for sale and see the price and expected costs before buying.
- [ ] Start with one player-owned business type, such as a small food shop or cafe.
- [ ] Add basic operations: stock, operating costs, and income from customers.
- [ ] Let owners work at or improve their business for better returns.
- [ ] Add business ownership and finances to the player save and Kallah transaction history.
- [ ] Prevent passive income from outpacing active jobs and gig rewards.
